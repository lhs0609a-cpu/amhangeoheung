## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished high fidelity Korean mobile app design presentation for 암행어흥, a mystery-shopping evidence-based local business review platform. This is an ORIGINAL brand, not a cosmetics app. Reference image is the existing tiger mascot; preserve its warm golden tiger and traditional Korean gat character design. Landscape presentation with THREE large readable flat phone screen panels on a warm cream background; restrained editorial typography, exceptional Korean text quality, generous 20px gutters, organized white cards, clean icons, very subtle crisp warm borders. Palette existing brand cream #FDF6E9, white #FFFFFF, golden ochre #F2B33D for actions with dark brown #3D2E1F text; cinnabar #D9482F ONLY unresolved findings; forest green ONLY confirmed improvement. Screen 1 consumer home: brand 암행어흥 top, title '좋은 가게를 고르는 새로운 기준', search '어떤 가게가 궁금하세요?', category chips '전체 음식점 카페 뷰티', compact mascot welcome banner, section '최근 공개된 감찰', balanced pros and cons review card labeled '좋았던 점' and '아쉬운 점', bottom tabs 홈 검색 랭킹 리뷰 마이. Screen 2 discovery: title '가게 찾기', search field '카페', category pills, result list with locality and review counts and action '감찰 기록 보기'; no giant scores or certification badges. Screen 3 evidence report: title '감찰 리포트', '예시 카페' below, note '디자인 예시', unresolved card '확인 대기 · 테이블 청결', merchant promise section '업체의 개선 약속', confirmed card '재감찰 확인 · 대기 안내', three-step timeline '지적 → 약속 → 재감찰', footer '좋았던 점과 아쉬운 점을 함께 확인해요'. No mascot on report screen. Small overall label '디자인 예시 · 실제 업체 데이터가 아닙니다'. These are clearly conceptual UI design mockups, not screenshots of a deployed app. Maintain mature trusted-information product quality comparable to leading Korean consumer review apps, retaining warm friendly tiger identity. Flat front-on screens, no tilted devices, no fake logos, no holography, no clutter.
